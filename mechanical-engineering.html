Mechanical Engineering Services

Voltoro offers a versatile range of mechanical engineering solutions that cater to complex industrial needs — supporting clients with design, analysis, manufacturing and maintenance of mechanical systems through both conventional and cutting-edge techniques.

  • Systems Design & Inspection
  • Equipment & System Fabrication
  • Maintenance & Reliability
01 — Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Systems Design and Inspection

Our mechanical systems design and inspection services ensure that industrial and commercial systems are designed, installed and maintained with the highest standards of safety, functionality and reliability. We offer comprehensive inspection services to ensure compliance with SEMA standards for warehouse pallet racks, supervise the construction and installation of new systems, and provide custom fabrication for various mechanical components tailored to meet your operational needs.

  • Mechanical systems design creating efficient, reliable solutions for industrial and commercial requirements.
  • Inspection of warehouse pallet racks to ensure compliance with SEMA standards.
  • Detailed assessments to identify weaknesses or areas for improvement before installation.
  • Construction supervision so all systems are installed to specification.
  • Quality assurance and quality control during installation of mechanical systems.
  • Custom fabrication of sheet metal enclosures, cabinets, kiosks, trolleys and racks.
  • Application of advanced CAD tools for precise design and simulation before implementation.
  • Fabrication of components that integrate seamlessly into existing systems.
  • Reliability and safety testing against industry standards and operational stresses.
  • Post-installation support and maintenance for ongoing efficiency and longevity.
02 — Mechanical Engineering

Industrial Equipment and System Fabrication

We specialize in the custom design, manufacturing and assembly of high-quality industrial machinery and systems. Advanced fabrication techniques — including welding, metalworking and assembly — deliver reliable, durable equipment tailored to the demands of your industry. From prototype creation to large-scale production, we ensure precision and quality at every stage.

  • Custom fabrication of industrial equipment to your operational and facility specifications.
  • Manufacturing of heavy-duty machinery for a range of industries.
  • Sheet metal fabrication for enclosures, cabinets, racks and other components.
  • Precision welding and assembly of components into robust, reliable systems.
  • Prototype development to test designs before mass production.
  • Tailored solutions for specific environmental, operational or regulatory needs.
  • Full-scale production services for large quantities of machinery and equipment.
  • Assembly of integrated electrical, mechanical and structural systems.
  • High-quality materials resistant to wear, corrosion and operational stresses.
  • Post-fabrication testing and quality control against safety and performance standards.
03 — Mechanical Engineering

Maintenance and Reliability Engineering

We focus on enhancing the lifespan, performance and reliability of your industrial systems and machinery through proactive maintenance planning, reliability assessments for critical equipment, and on-site repair for heavy industrial machinery. The goal is to minimise downtime, reduce maintenance costs and keep equipment operating efficiently over the long term.

  • Proactive maintenance planning and scheduling to reduce unplanned downtime.
  • Comprehensive reliability assessments for critical machinery.
  • On-site maintenance and repairs for heavy industrial equipment.
  • Condition-based monitoring to detect early signs of wear or malfunction.
  • Preventive maintenance programmes that extend machinery lifespan.
  • Failure mode analysis to evaluate potential failure points and develop mitigations.
  • Optimization of machinery and systems for performance, reliability and efficiency.
  • Emergency repair services to address sudden breakdowns with minimal disruption.
  • Data-driven maintenance using predictive analytics and monitoring tools.
  • Comprehensive maintenance documentation and reporting for compliance and planning.
