Electrical Engineering Services

Our electrical engineering consultancy is tailored for industrial clients, focusing on optimizing maintenance, efficiency and cost-effectiveness. We deliver solutions for planning, design, installation and maintenance of power transmission and distribution networks, meeting rigorous industry standards to ensure safety and reliability.

01 — Electrical Engineering

Industrial Power Systems and Compliance

Designed to ensure the safe, efficient and compliant operation of electrical systems in industrial settings. From designing power distribution networks to ensuring compliance with relevant standards, we provide tailored solutions that meet the specific energy demands of your facility while maintaining full regulatory adherence.

  • Design and installation of industrial power systems for specific operational and energy demands.
  • Power distribution system design for efficient, reliable electricity flow across facilities.
  • Compliance with national and international electrical safety standards.
  • Electrical system audits to identify inefficiencies or non-compliance areas.
  • Load balancing and optimization for efficient energy use and cost savings.
  • Power system monitoring and diagnostics to identify issues before they arise.
  • Backup power systems such as generators and UPS for continuity during outages.
  • Energy efficiency assessments to reduce consumption and operational costs.
  • Emergency power restoration services to minimise downtime after failures.
  • Documentation and reporting of compliance for audits and regulatory reviews.

03 — Electrical Engineering

Electrical Gap Analysis

Our gap analysis services identify weaknesses or inefficiencies in existing electrical systems. By assessing the current state of your infrastructure, we provide recommendations to bridge gaps, optimize performance and ensure your systems meet both operational and regulatory standards.

  • Comprehensive review of existing systems to identify inefficiencies, hazards and improvements.
  • Comparison of current systems against industry standards to pinpoint compliance gaps.
  • Energy efficiency analysis to identify opportunities for reducing consumption and costs.
  • Identification of non-compliant components with recommendations for updates or replacements.
  • Detailed performance evaluation to ensure systems meet operational needs.
  • Recommendations for system upgrades to improve reliability, capacity and compliance.
  • Audit of electrical safety practices to ensure compliance and minimise risk.
  • Assessment of maintenance practices to identify gaps in checks or necessary repairs.
  • Documentation of findings and action plans for bridging performance or compliance gaps.
  • Support with regulatory compliance against local and international standards.
