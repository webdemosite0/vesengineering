What kind of projects do you take on?

Industrial and commercial work across civil, mechanical and electrical scopes — from a single package such as epoxy flooring or a pallet rack inspection, through to a fully turnkey facility delivered end to end.

Can you handle regulatory approvals as well?

Yes. Our NOC compliance team works alongside the technical scope, handling documentation and liaison with government authorities through to final approval.

Do you work on existing facilities?

Regularly. Structural inspections and rehabilitation, electrical gap analysis, maintenance and reliability programmes and equipment upgrades are a core part of what we do.
